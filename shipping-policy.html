Shipping policy

Last updated: October 2026. Edit this text for your business and have it reviewed before going live.

Delivery areas

We deliver across Pakistan.

Delivery time

Major cities: 2–5 working days. Other areas: 4–7 working days. Delays can happen during sales and public holidays.

Charges

Delivery is free on orders of Rs. 5,000 or more. Below that a flat Rs. 250 applies.

Cash on delivery

Pay the courier when your parcel arrives. Please check the parcel before paying.

Tracking

We confirm every order by phone or message and share the courier tracking number once it ships.
